# Lean formalization

This project partially formalizes the proof in `proof/PROOF.md`. Its concrete target is `LocalHopf.SphereProduct.LocalHopfProblem` in `GeometricStatement.lean`. This proposition quantifies over smooth Riemannian metrics on Mathlib’s product of unit 2-spheres. It uses the Levi-Civita curvature numerator on orthonormal pairs and convergence of every order of derivative of the metric coefficients on compact subsets of product charts. The concrete theorem `BackgroundGrowth.Transverse.localHopfProblem_of_chart_tables` reduces this proposition to two explicit arithmetic identities. One identifies the minimized quadratic coefficient with the signed expression in Proposition 5.1 at every regular pair. The other identifies the uncorrected cubic coefficient with the exported forcing on the regular equatorial torus.

The proved theorem is conditional on `LocalHopfInput`. It combines pointwise vector and profile identities, the algebraic deduction of Proposition 5.2, compactness from regional lower bounds, and diagonal selection. The `tube` field supplies the regional estimates to this abstract theorem. A separate proof on compact normal charts derives the uniform two-sided expansion of the moving minimum from local $C^4$ regularity, quadratic background growth and vanishing first variation. Section 1’s compact incidence space, the density statements and the component identities have a concrete implementation, `FlatLocus.data`. For any smooth potential `χ`, Lean constructs the §2 tensors as metrics on a neighborhood of $s=0$ and expands the actual curvature numerator on moving graph planes at every fixed base point. For the longitude-height cubic exported by `equator_rotation.py`, Lean derives the rotation degree and mean, constructs the §6 potential and computes the change it makes to the intrinsic cubic on every equatorial tilt. It also proves smooth convergence of a diagonally chosen sequence of the actual scaled metrics. Lean verifies the quadratic interval certificate for the exported functions and expresses intrinsic curvature in the same longitude-height coordinates. Lean proves that the actual background curvature and its first variation vanish on the common-normal tori. Lean proves the frame cover, the background comparison, the changes of coordinates for both minimized coefficients, the torus correction, and the interval bound for the exported coefficients. The two arithmetic identities relating the intrinsic chart calculation to those exports remain open.

For each such input, the main theorem is

```lean
theorem local_hopf (I : LocalHopfInput) :
    ∃ x : ℕ → I.Met, (∀ n, I.PosSec (x n)) ∧ I.dist.TendstoCInf x I.gprod
```

There are no `axiom` declarations and no `sorry`. `#print axioms local_hopf` reports only Lean's standard `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` (see [`PrintAxioms.lean`](PrintAxioms.lean)).

## What is assumed

A concrete construction can use the structure instance syntax `{ FlatLocus.data with ... } : LocalHopfInput` to supply the flat-locus carrier, topology, weight, regular set and frame fields together. The general theorem also accepts other models. Its fields supply the carriers for metrics and planes, the product metric and curvature predicate, and the regular set, frame and coefficient functions. The following assumptions connect these data. The `tube` field already includes the lower bound after normal minimization. `positive_metrics_of_background_cover` gives a separate route to positivity on the sphere product. The concrete regular-locus reduction uses the proved chart estimates and two remaining identifications of coefficients.

| Field | Source in PROOF.md | Kind |

| `g`, `radius`, `G` | §1–§2: for each admissible $t$, a positive radius on which the scaled perturbation is a Riemannian metric | geometry |
| `ρ_cont`, `ρ_nonneg` | §1, Def. 3.2 and (H2): the weight on the flat locus is continuous and nonnegative | geometry |

## Outstanding formalization

The two conjuncts of `chartTablesMatch` remain to be proved for the paper data. Lean derives the normal Hessian from the background curvature, the four gradient entries from first-order curvature on pairs of fixed planes, and the fixed-plane quadratic coefficient from the second tensor and the first Christoffel symbols. For each of the two Chebyshev profile channels, Lean computes its contribution to the spatial curvature term as $18n_i^2[h(t)]^{-2}(u f'(u)+(u^2-1)f''(u))$.

- Proposition 5.1 requires the minimized quadratic coefficient to equal `exportedQuadratic` at every regular pair. The theorem `quadratic_table_iff_rational` cancels both Chebyshev profile terms exactly. Lean expresses the remaining equality using directional Taylor coefficients of the seven rational channels, the first-order tensor, the two-by-two solves and the signed entries of the export. The signed sine is positive at the canonical regular representative.
- Section 6 requires the minimized cubic coefficient before conformal correction to equal `EquatorJet.forcing` at each regular pair of equatorial longitudes. Once the quadratic vanishes, `equator_cubic_iff_numerator` reduces this to the ordinary cubic numerator coefficient times $4hD$. The comparison uses the signed separation and rotation half-angles and includes the rotation by $\pi$ omitted by a finite tangent parameter.
